The name at the top of the home page isn't an image or a font. Each letter's outline is traced, turned into a loop of rotating circles with a Fourier transform, and redrawn from a limited number of those circles. Fewer circles give softer shapes.

Three inks are printed on top of each other, each with its own number of harmonics, a little out of register, the way a risograph prints.

## Why bother

Mostly because it's fun. Open **Print settings** under the wordmark to play with it; your choices stay in your browser.
